# Privacy Policy for Voltwise

Effective date: September 13, 2026

Voltwise respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how Voltwise handles information when you use the app.

## Information Voltwise handles
Voltwise reads battery and charging information provided by Android, such as battery percentage, charging status, charger connection state, temperature, voltage, current, wattage estimates, charging sessions and related battery events.

## How information is used
This information is used to show live battery readings, charging graphs, charging speed estimates, battery health insights and optional battery alerts.

## Local storage
Voltwise stores battery history locally on your device so charts, charging sessions and insights can work over time.

## Sharing and cloud transfer
Voltwise does not require an account and does not send your battery history to a cloud server. Voltwise does not sell personal data.

## Notifications
Voltwise uses notifications for foreground monitoring and optional battery alerts such as charge target, low battery, high temperature and optional daily reports. You can control alert settings inside the app and Android system notification settings.

## Permissions
Voltwise may request notification permission so alerts can be shown. Foreground service permission is used for live battery monitoring. Wake lock may be used while charging to keep monitoring reliable.

## Children
Voltwise is not designed specifically for children.

## Data deletion
You can clear recorded battery history in the app settings. You can also remove all local app data through Android system settings by clearing app storage or uninstalling the app.

## Contact
For privacy questions, contact the support email listed on the Voltwise Google Play Store listing.

## Changes
This policy may be updated when the app changes. The latest version should be available from the app listing or support page.
