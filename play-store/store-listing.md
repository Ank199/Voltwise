### Full description
Voltwise helps you understand how your phone charges in real time.

See your charging speed, voltage, wattage, battery heat, charge history and estimated time to full in a simple premium dashboard. Voltwise turns technical battery readings into easy guidance, so you know when charging is fast, slow, warm, healthy or ready to unplug.

Key features:
- Live charging dashboard with current, voltage, wattage and temperature
- Battery percentage over time graph
- Custom charge target alert: choose the battery percent where you want a notification
- Low battery and high temperature alerts
- Charge time estimate and charging speed history
- Average and best recorded charging speed
- Battery health and charging care insights
- Optional daily battery report
- Local history stored on your device
- No login required

Voltwise is useful for checking chargers, cables, heat, charging habits and battery care. Sensor availability depends on your Android device. If a device does not expose a reading, Voltwise clearly marks it as unsupported instead of showing fake values.

Privacy-first design:
Voltwise does not require an account. Battery samples and history are stored locally on your device.
